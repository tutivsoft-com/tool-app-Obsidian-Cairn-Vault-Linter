# Top 10 Benefits of Cairn Vault Linter

1. **Find broken references** — Check wikilinks, Markdown links, embeds, headings, block IDs, and aliases.
2. **Scan at the right scope** — Review the full vault, current note, current folder, or changed notes.
3. **See why a result was reported** — Findings include source and target context to support manual verification.
4. **Stay in control of the scan** — Follow progress and cancel a long-running scan.
5. **Focus the report** — Filter results by type, severity, folder, and ignored state.
6. **Keep known exceptions visible** — Ignore findings with a reason instead of losing track of them.
7. **Export useful reports** — Save reviewed results as Markdown, CSV, or JSON.
8. **Preview supported repairs** — Inspect exact duplicate-link changes before applying them.
9. **Recover from a repair** — Cairn keeps a journal for rollback of the latest eligible batch.
10. **Keep vault content local** — Scanning and billing do not upload note contents; billing uses separate account and usage metadata.
